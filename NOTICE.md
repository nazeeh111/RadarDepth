# Source and additions

The retained implementation comes from [rpl-cmu/learned-depth-imaging-radar](https://github.com/rpl-cmu/learned-depth-imaging-radar/tree/b2d3e4edd16b9d5b3c8765201494b97d857d8158), snapshot `b2d3e4edd16b9d5b3c8765201494b97d857d8158`. The source is associated with the IROS 2022 learned radar-depth method; the named authors are Ruoyang Xu, Wei Dong, Akash Sharma and Michael Kaess.

RadarDepth adds a command-line facade, source/CPU parity checks and setup documentation, maintained by nazeeh111. These additions do not establish new authorship of the research method or independently reproduce the source-reported scientific results.

The 2022 Robot Perception Lab MIT notice remains in [LICENSE](LICENSE), alongside the separate new-contribution notice. Bundled ColoRadar tooling and calibration assets retain their terms. The source research citation is preserved in [the reference guide](docs/REFERENCE.md). Random-weight CPU parity is not trained depth accuracy.
